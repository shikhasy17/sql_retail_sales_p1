# Retail Sales | SQL + Analytics Project
## Executive Summary
Built SQL analytics project to transform raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights.
Identified revenue drivers, customer segments, and time-based sales trends to support data-driven decision-making.
________________________________________
## Business Objectives
•	Identify top-performing product categories
•	Analyze customer demographics & behavior
•	Detect seasonality and sales patterns
•	Highlight high-value customers for targeting
________________________________________
## Tech Stack
•	SQL (PostgreSQL)
•	Excel (Data Cleaning & Validation)
•	Power BI (Dashboarding)
________________________________________
## Dataset Overview
•	Transactions-level retail dataset
•	Features: date, time, customer demographics, category, quantity, pricing
•	Cleaned and validated before analysis
________________________________________
## Data Pipeline
1.	Data imported from CSV into PostgreSQL
2.	Data cleaning (null handling, type corrections)
3.	Exploratory analysis using SQL
4.	Business queries built for insight extraction
5.	Visualization in Power BI
________________________________________
## Key Insights & Business Impact
# Revenue Optimization
•	Clothing category contributes highest share of total revenue
•	High-value transactions (>1000) reveal premium customer segment
Business Action: Focus marketing & inventory on high-performing categories
________________________________________
## Customer Segmentation
•	Female customers contribute significantly to total sales
•	Top 5 customers account for a disproportionate share of revenue
Business Action: Launch loyalty programs for high-value customers
________________________________________
## Time-Based Trends
•	Monthly analysis reveals clear seasonality patterns
•	Peak sales occur during specific periods
Business Action: Plan promotions & stock for peak demand cycles
________________________________________
## Advanced SQL Concepts Used
•	Aggregations: SUM, AVG, COUNT
•	Window Functions: RANK() OVER()
•	Date Functions: EXTRACT(), TO_CHAR()
•	CTEs (Common Table Expressions)
•	Data Cleaning Queries
•	Business KPI calculations
